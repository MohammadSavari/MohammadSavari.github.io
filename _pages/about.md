Currently, I am pursuing my PhD in the captivating field of network science, focusing on understanding the fundamental mechanisms of how ideas and behaviors spread through complex networks. I conduct this research under the guidance of Professor <a href="https://orcid.org/0000-0002-2507-4642" target="_blank">Roland Bouffanais</a> at the <a href="https://applied-complexity.org/" target="_blank">Applied Complexity Group</a>.

My work sits at the intersection of computational social science, network theory, and behavioral dynamics, exploring how information cascades through interconnected systems and influences collective behavior.
