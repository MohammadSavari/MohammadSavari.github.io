I am a Ph.D. candidate in network science, studying the fundamental mechanisms by which ideas and behaviors spread through complex networks. I conduct this research under the supervision of Professor <a href="https://orcid.org/0000-0002-2507-4642" target="_blank">Roland Bouffanais</a> at the <a href="https://applied-complexity.org/" target="_blank">Applied Complexity Group</a>, University of Ottawa. I also work as a Complex Networks Researcher at the <a href="https://telfer.uottawa.ca/en/" target="_blank">Telfer School of Management</a>, collaborating with Professor <a href="https://telfer.uottawa.ca/en/directory/jose-carlos-marques/" target="_blank">José Carlos Marques</a> on a whole-network analysis of voluntary sustainability standards organizations (VSSO).

My work sits at the intersection of computational social science, network theory, and control/system dynamics, developing models of social contagion and consensus formation to explain how information cascades through interconnected systems and shapes collective behavior. Alongside my research, I work as a Project Manager at the Centre for Entrepreneurship and Engineering Design, mentoring student teams through the full engineering design cycle, and as a Teaching Assistant supporting courses in mechatronics, systems dynamics, and robotics.
