CEED

Accessibility design projects I have managed as Project Manager at the University of Ottawa's Centre for Entrepreneurship and Engineering Design (CEED).

Depth Perception Assist

A wearable device giving audible distance feedback for a client with vision in only one eye.

One-Handed Switch Controller

An adaptive Nintendo Switch controller attachment for a client who can only use one hand.

Guitar Strum Assist

A device that supports a client's hand in position to strum an acoustic guitar despite limited mobility on one side.

Wheelchair Rocking Board — Play Therapy

A rocking board letting children in wheelchairs roll on and enjoy a new type of movement.

Voice Recognition for Home Assistants

Software to help users with unclear speech get recognized by devices like Alexa and Google Home.

Ambu Bag Squeeze

A device that independently operates a lung volume recruitment bag for a client without caregiver help.

Switch-Activated Wheelchair Rocking Board

A 300-lb-rated rocking platform, activated by an adapted switch, for children with complex disabilities.

Portable Wheelchair Ramp

A lightweight, transportable ramp letting a wheelchair user clear a single step independently.

Head Support

A wheelchair-mounted headrest that keeps a user's head upright and alerts when it tilts out of position.

Arm-Supported Cup Holder

An arm-mounted holder that lets a mobility-aid user carry and drink from a travel mug one-handed.

Mobile Feed Pump Mount

A portable mount that installs a feeding-tube pump in different locations, like a car headrest or wheelchair, for travel.

Cup Flow Limiter

A flow-restricting insert for a Nosey Cup that slows liquid flow to prevent spills and choking risk.

Accessible Handle Grip

An adjustable-angle handle grip that redirects a spoon or paintbrush to a comfortable angle for a bent wrist.

Secure Cup Holder

A cup holder that stays securely fixed to a wheelchair tray, waterproof and rugged against sudden movement.
