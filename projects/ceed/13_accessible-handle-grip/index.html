Accessible Handle Grip

An adjustable-angle handle grip that redirects a spoon or paintbrush to a comfortable angle for a bent wrist.

Two teams independently tackled the same client brief.

Client brief: The client’s daughter wants to take part in daily activities independently, but her wrist bends inward, making it hard to hold a spoon or paintbrush at the right angle. The task was to design a two-part device — an ergonomic handle plus a gripper that redirects the angle of the held item — lightweight, easy to clean, and adjustable to different object sizes.

Teams & solutions:

  • Accessible Handle Grip (Group AHG1 — PONYY)
  • Handl — a grip with a flexible inner core that adapts to the user’s hand shape and a smart stabilization system for adaptive angle control.
