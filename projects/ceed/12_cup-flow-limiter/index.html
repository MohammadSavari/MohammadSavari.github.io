Cup Flow Limiter

A flow-restricting insert for a Nosey Cup that slows liquid flow to prevent spills and choking risk.

Three teams independently tackled the same client brief.

Client brief: The client’s daughter uses a Nosey Cup so she doesn’t have to tilt her head back as far when drinking, but liquid still flows out too fast and spills. The task was to design a device compatible with the Nosey Cup (or a new cup achieving the same result) that limits liquid flow, is washable, rugged, and easy to install, ideally with an adjustable opening.

Teams & solutions:

  • Nosey Cup Hat — a silicone-cast specialized lid with a flow-control opening that clips onto the existing Nosey Cup rim.
  • The Drippy Cup — a 2-piece 3D-printed cup with a built-in flow limiter in the lid, based on the Nosey Cup design.
  • Flowtech Industries — CFL3 — a flow limiter that attaches to an existing 8 oz Nosey Cup while preserving its profile.
