As a Complex Networks Researcher at the [Telfer School of Management](https://telfer.uottawa.ca/en/), University of Ottawa, I work with [Professor José Carlos Marques](https://telfer.uottawa.ca/en/directory/jose-carlos-marques/) on his research project, *"Examining the global sustainability standards organizational landscape: A whole network analysis,"* funded by a Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council (SSHRC) Insight Development Grant.

**Project background:** Voluntary Sustainability Standards Organizations (VSSOs) are the certifications and labels — from Fair Trade to forest-stewardship schemes — that claim to address social and environmental issues across global supply chains. Their rapid, uncoordinated growth has produced inconsistency and, at times, greenwashing. The project builds a whole-network dataset of VSSOs to understand how they form, structure themselves, and grow their membership and influence within the global system of sustainability standardization.

**My role:**

- Identify and characterize sub-networks, communities, and topological properties within this complex global network using network science methods.
- Collect, clean, and integrate data through web scraping, natural language processing, manual extraction, and entity-matching protocols.
- Investigate the mechanisms driving network formation, evolution, and inter-organizational relationships among VSSOs.
- Develop network visualizations and computational models using Python and Gephi.
- Analyze collaboration patterns and classify organizational roles — including NGOs, multinational corporations, and non-state actors — within these interconnected alliance networks.
