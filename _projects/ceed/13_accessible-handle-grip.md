As Project Manager at the Centre for Entrepreneurship and Engineering Design (CEED), University of Ottawa, I oversaw this GNG2101 accessibility design project, in which two teams independently tackled the same client brief.

**Client brief:** The client's daughter wants to take part in daily activities independently, but her wrist bends inward, making it hard to hold a spoon or paintbrush at the right angle. The task was to design a two-part device — an ergonomic handle plus a gripper that redirects the angle of the held item — lightweight, easy to clean, and adjustable to different object sizes.
