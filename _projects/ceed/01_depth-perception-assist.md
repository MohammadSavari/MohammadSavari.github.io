**Client brief:** The client has vision in only one eye and no depth perception, making it difficult to judge distances to objects — especially during exercise, when picking items up and putting them down quickly. The team's task was to design a device that gives an audible indication of distance without encumbering movement, robust and easy to clean.

**Team & solution:**

- [Proxi-Glove: Depth Perception Assistant](https://makerepo.com/raguiabah/2868.depth-perception-assist)
