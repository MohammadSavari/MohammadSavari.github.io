As Project Manager at the Centre for Entrepreneurship and Engineering Design (CEED), University of Ottawa, I oversaw this GNG2101 accessibility design project, in which two teams independently tackled the same client brief.

**Client brief:** The client has an acoustic guitar they would like to play again but has limited movement on one side. The task was to design a device that supports the client's hand in position to strum the guitar, installable and operable with one hand, without damaging the instrument.

**Teams & solutions:**

- [Still Strummin' — Guitar Strum Assist](https://makerepo.com/mat404/2840.gng2101-still-strummin-guitar-strum-assist) — an adjustable one-handed mounting solution built around off-the-shelf hardware (lazy susan bearing, suction mounts).
